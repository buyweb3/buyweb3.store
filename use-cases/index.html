What can a Web3 domain do?

A Web3 domain can provide a readable identity for payments, profiles, communities and decentralized content, depending on extension and platform support.

Browse domains

Readable payment identity

A supported Web3 name can map to cryptocurrency addresses, helping people use a memorable name rather than a long wallet string.

Decentralized websites

Some Web3 domains can point to content stored on decentralized networks. Browser and resolver support varies.

Profiles and communities

A name can become a public identity for a founder, collector, fan community, campaign or creative project.
