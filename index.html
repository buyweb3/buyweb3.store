About BuyWeb3

BuyWeb3 was founded by Mark Leen, an independent entrepreneur, writer and collector of distinctive Web3 domain names. The portfolio was assembled over several years through extensive research, creative naming and personal investment.

The marketplace brings those names together for founders, communities, collectors and fans seeking a memorable digital identity. Every listing is independently owned and presented at an asking price; availability and transfer details are confirmed before payment.

Purchase requests and offers are handled directly through the BuyWeb3 WhatsApp contact while availability, payment and transfer details are confirmed.
